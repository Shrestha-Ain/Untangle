## PROMPT FOR FRONTEND DESIGN GENERATION

These are the finalized project decisions and frontend design for Untangle — a knowledge mapping tool that turns research papers and textbooks into interactive, queryable knowledge graphs.

**Project name:** Untangle

**Two feature modes:**
- Research Mode: Upload an academic paper — extracts entities, relationships, communities — renders as isometric knowledge map
- Study Mode: Upload a textbook/book — extracts chapter → section → topic → subtopic hierarchy — renders on the same isometric canvas with adapted labels

**Path-Based Learning Traversal:**
- The knowledge map remains an interconnected network, but overlays a sequential curriculum/learning path (Step 1 → Step 2 → Step 3...).
- Includes an interactive Guided Journey HUD (`LearningPathStepper.vue`) with [Prev] Step X of N [Next] controls that auto-select and spotlight concepts in recommended pedagogical order.
- Illuminated dashed path trail connects nodes sequentially on the SVG canvas.

**Deep Topic Reader ("Read Everything Related to That Topic"):**
- Every tower/concept features an option to open a comprehensive deep reader drawer/modal (`TopicReaderModal.vue`).
- Displays 2-min exam takeaways, executive concept synthesis, key equations/code blocks, all verbatim raw chunks from the source document, and prerequisite pathways.

**Exam Gist & High-Yield Revision Sheet:**
- Designed for time-pressured exam cramming when students have minutes to review.
- Document-Wide Sheet (`ExamGistModal.vue`): Accessible from the top HUD ("⚡ Exam Gist") and dashboard map cards. Aggregates and ranks the most critical/high-yield topics with rapid 2-sentence summaries, memorization formulas, and common exam traps, plus "Jump to Tower on Map" quick-actions.
- Per-Topic Exam Takeaways (`TopicReaderModal.vue`): Top-of-page quick takeaways banner giving the essence, formula, and classic exam question at a glance before diving into raw chunks.

**Map UI Design (Inspired by page2 prototype, strictly NO flags):**
- High-fidelity 2.5D architectural SVG buildings:
  - Concept / Spire: 3D stepped faceted crystal body, floating crystal gem with soft glow, pulsing base rings, sky light beam.
  - Author / Lodge: Cozy steepled Victorian research academy with shaded isometric walls and arch entry door (NO flags).
  - Location / Arena: Multi-tiered base plinth + golden training dome.
  - Chapter / Citadel: Grand multi-tiered academic citadel.
  - Section / Modern Block: Layered tech block with solar/glass panels and bevels.
- Triple-layered roads with underlays, pastel surface lanes, and animated particle streams.
- Multi-polygon pastel turf platforms for communities and chapters.

**Dashboard UI Design (Inspired by page1 prototype):**
- Smooth and lively aesthetic with soft atmospheric glows (`blur-3xl`), tactile borders (`border-2 border-[#D4D3CE]`), live sync pulse tags, animated upload icon float, and interactive mini-tag preview strips on map cards.

**AI guide:** Regulus — a neutral, mode-aware AI companion (not gamified, not character-named per-mode).

**No gamification:** No XP, levels, sparks, energy, quests, player profiles, or any game mechanics.

**Color system:** Muted, warm, academic palette. Warm off-white surfaces (#F7F6F3). Desaturated node colors: dusty lavender (#7C6FA0), warm ochre (#C49A3C), muted jade (#4E9A7D), dusty rose-red (#A85A5A), dusty slate-blue (#4E8FA8). Primary brand: muted forest green (#3D6B5A).

**Fonts:** Inter (all UI text) + JetBrains Mono (labels, data, badges). No game fonts.

**Auth:** Clerk via @clerk/vue. No custom JWT.

**Cross-source linking:** Both modes support auto-suggest (Regulus flags when a concept needs outside context — Qdrant suggests related docs from user library) AND manual import (user uploads another source at any time from inside the map).

These decisions apply to all future frontend code, UI generation, and design references for this project.
